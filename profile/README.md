# CLUSTER

**A student community around compute.** GPUs, systems, applied AI and the people who make it run.

Good things happen in clusters. Every project worth talking about on campus started with a few people who found each other. We give those people a place to meet, a DGX to run on, and a reason to ship.

## Circles

| Circle | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Compute** | Everything that runs on the DGX H200: CUDA kernels, Slurm and multi-node MPI, LLM fine-tuning and TensorRT-LLM. |
| **Applied AI** | Models that leave the rack: computer vision and 3D, Jetson and Isaac ROS robotics, Omniverse digital twins. |
| **Crew** | Design, media, outreach and events. The people who make the club visible and make the day run. |
